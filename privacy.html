Privacy policy

AiSmartCot · effective September 25, 2026

Who uses it

AiSmartCot is a personal assistant for one person (the owner). There are no other users.

Google data used and why

With the owner's permission, AiSmartCot accesses the owner's Google Calendar and Google Tasks only to create, update and delete the events and tasks the owner asked for, to see which tasks the owner marked as completed, and to show the owner's events in the morning summary.

Where data is stored

The owner's notes and sync metadata are stored on the owner's own phone and in the owner's encrypted backup. For speech recognition and note parsing, text and audio are sent to the Google Gemini API.

Sharing

None. Data is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with third parties other than the Google services listed above. AiSmartCot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

At any time: myaccount.google.com/permissions → AiSmartCot → remove access.
