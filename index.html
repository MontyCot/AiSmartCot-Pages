AiSmartCot

A personal voice assistant for a single user.

AiSmartCot is a Telegram bot that takes its owner's voice and text notes, sorts them by topic and, on request, adds events to the owner's Google Calendar and tasks to the owner's Google Tasks.

It is not a public service: only the owner uses it, and there is no sign-up for anyone else.
